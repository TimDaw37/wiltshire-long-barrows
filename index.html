About this map

Interactive gazetteer of Wiltshire Neolithic long barrows from open HER extracts and Historic England scheduling polygons, plus Tim Daw’s modern All Cannings long barrow. Terrain is a county-wide EA Composite DTM hillshade at ~20 m; desktop zoom shows 1 m chips. Cotswold–Severn (stone-chambered) vs earthen is the main type split — peer-cited membership only.

Gold = HER certain · grey = possible. Circle markers: gold = HER certain, grey = possible, teal = modern. Rim arrows = undirected long-axis bearings from north (0–180°), human-checked where shown. Gold outline = ceremonial Wiltshire (UA + Swindon).
LiDAR: County backdrop: EA Composite DTM ~20 m (all devices). Per-barrow 1 m chips: desktop map zoom ≳14, and in the detail strip when a barrow is selected.

Long axis

Map arrows are undirected long-axis bearings from north (0–180°), human-checked where shown. They are not solar alignments or façade directions.

Cotswold–Severn vs earthen

Cotswold–Severn = stone-chambered; earthen = Wessex chalk mounds (timber chambers if any). Neolithic Cotswold–Severn membership is peer-cited only (Darvill / Corcoran / Crawford / excavations / HE). All Cannings is included as a modern (2014–) stone-chambered long barrow in Cotswold–Severn form — not a Neolithic HER site.

Known limits

  • Many HER rows lack published names; display falls back to HER / parish number.
  • Long-axis bearings are human-checked where shown; sites without a clear axis appear as plain circles.
  • Length/width from scheduling polygons are approximate (often oversize vs mound).
  • Not a complete county inventory of every ploughed / cropmark candidate.
  • Cotswold–Severn membership is curated from peer sources; fringe / dubious chamber claims stay earthen unless cited.
